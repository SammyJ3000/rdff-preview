What we run

Eight classes

Whatever shape you’re in right now, one of these fits. Not sure which? Come to any of them free and we’ll point you at the right one.

01

RD FF

Our signature circuit class. Circuit training done properly — intelligent, varied and effective programming.

02

RD Smooth

A lower intensity version of RD FF. Perfect if you’re starting out, getting back into exercise, or you’d prefer a more moderate challenge.

03

RD Hybrid

Fitness racing inspired — Hyrox, ATHX, DEKA. Improve fitness, build strength and get race ready.

04

RD Pump

Our signature barbell class. A full body workout using weights, lifting at specific tempos. Muscular conditioning — strengthen and tone.

05

RD Fierce

High Intensity Interval Training. Short bursts of intense work with brief recovery. Enhances fat burning and cardiovascular health.

06

RD Engine

Cardiovascular conditioning. Unleash the benefits of our ergs as you run, ride, row and ski your way towards the best version of you.

07

RD Nurture

Our mum and baby class. An effective postnatal session focused on restorative movement — rebuild core strength, improve posture and boost wellbeing. Babies welcome.

08

RD Run Club

All abilities welcome. Varied routes, social and supporting. Runs every three weeks.
